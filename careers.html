HR Manager

Abu Dhabi HQ
Apply Now

Avanti Contracting is seeking a strategic and people-oriented HR Manager to lead our Human Resources department from our Head Office in Abu Dhabi. As we continue to expand our footprint in the UAE infrastructure and energy sectors, we need a professional who can align our HR functions with our business goals, ensuring we attract, develop, and retain the best technical talent in the industry.

Key Requirements

  • Experience: Minimum 5–8 years of HR management in UAE Construction or Engineering sectors
  • Compliance: Deep knowledge of UAE Labor Law, Abu Dhabi visa processes (TAMM/ICP), and MOHRE
  • Nationalization: Solid awareness of Emiratisation targets and commitment to national quotas
  • Skills: Proven ability in strategic engineering recruitment
  • Nationality: Open to all nationalities

How to Apply

Submit your CV to careers@avanti-uae.com with subject line "HR Manager - Abu Dhabi HQ"
