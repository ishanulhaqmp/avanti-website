About Avanti Contracting

Our Story

Established in 2010 in the great nation of the United Arab Emirates, Avanti Contracting™ is headquartered in the Capital City, Abu Dhabi.

Avanti™ possesses profound experience in the Electrical, Civil, Structural, and Mechanical fields, while boasting leading comprehensive expertise in the infrastructure domain. Also provided are meticulous specialized services in Instrumentation, Automation, and Testing.

Whether it be Engineering, Procurement, Construction or Maintenance, the dynamic team of directors, engineers, and technicians at Avanti™, along with our skilled labor force, ensure world-class project delivery — on time, every time.

"The true beauty of a project does not lie in its final appearance but rather in its infrastructure. For it will stand the test of time."

Our Direction

Vision Statement

"To become a regional leader with diversified business to cover all aspects of the building construction industry, providing value for time, money, quality products and superior services for our customers."

To be recognized nationally as the contractor of choice for all electrical, building and maintenance needs, delivering bespoke client-focused solutions.

To aspire to be the most recognized and utilized commercial contracting & general maintenance firm in the region.

To be considered an industry "Best-in-Class" contractor through financial discipline, innovative processes and best practices.

To build projects ahead of time, under budget and with satisfied customers who are proud of the finished product.

To create business relationships for life and be the preferred employer in the contracting industry.

Our Purpose

Mission Statement

To perform for our customers the highest level of quality contracting, sub-contracting & general maintenance services at fair and market competitive prices.

To ensure the longevity of our company through repeat and referral business achieved by customer satisfaction in all areas including timeliness and attention to detail.

To maintain the highest levels of professionalism, integrity, honesty and fairness in our relationships with contractors, suppliers, subcontractors and customers.

To pledge and establish lasting relationships with our customers by exceeding their expectations and gaining their trust through exceptional performance.

To provide our employees with an honest and helpful working environment where every employee can dedicate themselves to delivering exceptional workmanship.

To take great pride in our accomplishments and provide a professional approach to contracting exceptional quality projects which meet budget and schedule goals.

What Drives Us

Our Values

Integrity

Avanti™ will act honestly and with integrity, acknowledging the dependence of its success on trusting relationships with its clients. We operate with high regard for the environment, the community, and the people with whom we work.

Safety

Avanti™ will not compromise in providing employees and others under its care with a safe and healthy working environment. Ensuring that everyone is sent home safely at the end of each working day — making safety a competitive advantage.

Teamwork

Avanti™ is one team, and recognizes that its success is only possible through the collaborative efforts of its people. We develop people through training, reward performance and create a working environment where the team is challenged and motivated.

Innovation

We embrace modern technologies in automation, IT and smart infrastructure — continually improving our services to meet and exceed client expectations across every project we deliver.
